<header class="page-header"><h1>Your first Horo analysis</h1><p>You can start with an existing workout. Keep this guide handy for your first visit.</p></header>
<div class="hero-actions"><a class="btn btn-primary" href="{{ site.app_store_url }}">Download free on the App Store</a><a class="btn btn-secondary" href="#next-workout">Already started?</a></div>
<article class="prose"><h2>1. Check your starting point</h2><p>Use an iPhone or iPad on iOS / iPadOS 26 or later. Check that a workout appears in Apple Health. If it was recorded on your watch, let it sync first.</p></article>
<article class="prose"><h2>2. Give Horo access</h2><p>Open Horo and follow setup. Allow reading of workouts and relevant metrics in the Health permission prompt. If you declined, review Horo’s access in Apple Health’s app permissions.</p></article>
<article class="prose"><h2>3. Read your first analysis</h2><p>Choose your coach, then open a recent workout. Stay connected to the internet while the analysis loads. Start with one session you remember well so you can judge whether the feedback matches your experience.</p></article>
<article class="prose"><h2>4. Pick one useful takeaway</h2><p>What did the feedback help you understand? Choose one point relevant to your goal. You do not need to change everything at once.</p></article>
<article class="prose" id="next-workout"><h2>After your next workout</h2><p>Once the new session appears in Apple Health, open it in Horo. Read the feedback alongside your own experience. Compare similar sessions and conditions; one number or one session is not enough to judge progress. Bookmark this guide if it helps you remember.</p></article>
<article class="prose"><h2>No workouts or missing metrics?</h2><p>First check Apple Health, then Horo’s permissions. Not every device or workout records every metric. If the analysis fails, check your connection and remaining allowance in the app before trying again.</p></article>
